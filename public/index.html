The building

A new landmark for Oniru's commercial district

Trinity Towers brings striking architecture and considered interiors to the heart of Oniru, Victoria Island, one of the fastest-growing commercial areas in Lagos.

As the name says, it is three towers in one building: the North, Central and South Towers rise together over 12 floors of commercial and leisure space. It is a mixed-use development owned by City of David Church (Redeemed Christian Church of God), and the first building of its kind in West Africa.
